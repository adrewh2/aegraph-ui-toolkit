# `src/features` — the pages and applets

One folder per page or applet. Each is a projection over the ES (`src/core`): it reads the store with hooks, writes through it, and keeps only view state of its own. Routes are in `src/app/AppRoutes.tsx`; the sidebar in `src/components/Sidebar` (its fold-to-rail state is the shared `rail.ts`, so a view can fold it — the explorer's Full view does; its pages and their names are `nav.tsx`, which `pageTitle.ts` reads for the browser tab's title — a new page reached from elsewhere than the sidebar goes in its `otherPages`); the standard table, inspector and dialogs in `resources/` and `src/components/ui`.

## Conventions

- **A view's keys are listed in its corner.** A view that answers to keys (Unigraph's camera, a diagram's pan and zoom) says so with `components/KeysHint` in its bottom-right corner, and leaves a key alone when `lib/keys.ts › keyIsElsewhere` says a field, a menu or a dialog has it. A key held glides (one frame loop while any is down, moving by the time since the last frame — `useCameraKeys`, `diagrams/usePreviewKeys.ts`), and Shift makes it fast.
- **A write costs what it touches, on every page.** A chat turn writes its record; with the WildChat atlas open (489,700 imported conversations, a million entities) that write froze the tab for 0.5–0.9 s on whatever page the chat was, because views far from it recomputed over the whole project. So: the Chats page's rows are a per-store index kept by the store's events (`chats/useChatRows.ts › ChatIndex`: rows patched by binary search on date, grouped as the list shows them — never a re-list and re-sort of every conversation); Unigraph's slice keys on the writes that can change it (`graph/explorerState.tsx › affectsSlice`, `useSliceVersion`: a node in the view, one its `only`/`types` could admit, a link to one, a removal of one — anything the filter cannot rule out at a glance counts), its styles on the ontology and tag colours rather than the registry's identity, and **a hidden view does not follow data at all** (`useWhileShown`, `useViewActive`: it catches up once when shown). A new view over the whole project follows the same rule: key on what can change it, and freeze while hidden.
- **An entity's right-click menu is one menu.** Wherever an entity is right-clicked — a table row (`EntityTable`), a Unigraph node or edge (`graph/GraphMenu.tsx`), a map feature (`maps/MapWorkbench.tsx › MapStage`, through `MapCanvas`'s and `GlobeStage`'s `onFeatureMenu`) — the menu is `resources/entityMenu.tsx › useEntityMenu`: the view's own items as `lead`, then the standard items in a fixed order, the dialogs they open (Rename, Edit, Annotate, Link, Set property, delete confirmation) held by the hook and rendered once by the view (`dialogs`). A view with its own deletion (a folder's cascade, the graph's confirmation) passes `onDelete`. A new place an entity can be right-clicked uses the hook; it does not grow a menu of its own.
- **What floats closes through `useDismiss`.** A menu, dropdown, popover, picker, flyout or dialog closes on a press outside it and on Escape through `components/ui/useDismiss.ts` — never a `mousedown` listener of its own on the document. The hook hears the press as `pointerdown` in the capture phase (a dialog, a canvas or Leaflet can stop or cancel it on the way; the Dialog's box once did, and a dropdown in the New type dialog never closed), keeps the open layers as a stack (a press in a popup opened from another counts as inside both; Escape closes the topmost only), and takes an `inside` list (the popup and its opening button, so that button's click closes it rather than reopening it). `Menu`, `Popover`, `Select`, `Dialog`, `ColorPick`, `EntityPicker`, the sidebar's flyout and the scene menu use it; `components/ui/__tests__/dismissGuard.test.ts` fails on a listener of one's own.
- **What is modal keeps the keyboard.** A dialog is `components/ui/Dialog`; anything else modal (the `⌘K` palette) calls `components/ui/useFocusTrap` on its box: focus in on open (`autoFocus`, `data-autofocus`, else the first control), Tab and Shift+Tab wrap inside, focus back to the opener on close. A combobox over a list of results keeps the focus in its input and names the active result with `aria-activedescendant` (the palette's pattern: `role="combobox"`, `role="listbox"`, `role="option"` with `aria-selected`). A dropdown is a menu of radio items (`Menu` items with `checked`), never `aria-haspopup="listbox"` over a `role="menu"`.
- **Motion from script asks first.** CSS transitions and animations are cut by one `prefers-reduced-motion` rule in `src/index.css`; what moves from script — a camera flight (the engine's `Camera` / `OrbitCamera.animateTo` cut for it), a smooth `scrollBy` / `scrollIntoView` (`behavior: scrollBehavior()`) — asks `lib/motion.ts › prefersReducedMotion` and jumps.
- **Scrollbars are thin and stay in view.** A panel, rail, list, menu, dialog or page body that scrolls gets the app's 3 px scrollbar, visible whenever there is more to see: add its class to the list in `src/index.css` (or give the element `.thin-scroll`). Never set `scrollbar-width` / `scrollbar-color` outside `@supports not selector(::-webkit-scrollbar)` — in Chrome and Safari either one switches the thin bar back to an overlay that fades. Code, editors, data tables and grids keep the native bar (too thin to drag sideways).
- **A crash stays where it happened.** `src/components/ErrorBoundary` wraps the sidebar, the page (reset when the path changes) and the assistant panel in `AppLayout`, and every workstation pane: a part that throws shows a note with *Try again* and *Reload page* in its place and the rest keeps working. `isStaleCode` tells a crash only a reload fixes (a deploy's missing chunk; on the dev server a hot-swap that changed a mounted component's hooks) and says so. A new region of the layout that renders on its own gets its own boundary.
- A page's state that other views need (selection, what is visible, the playhead) lives in one hook or handle — `useMapWorkbench`, `explorerHandle` — so the stage, the table, the timeline and the tools all read the same thing. Give it stable identities: a memo that returns the previous array when membership is unchanged, `useCallback` for what a memoised child receives.
- Tables are always `EntityTable`; give it `storageKey`, `fields` from `registry.fieldsFor(type)`, and `defaultSort`/`defaultFilters` from the layout in force. Do not write a second table.
- Detail panes are the `Inspector`; context menus are `Menu` items; long operations report through the toast and a job bar.
- Stages that stay mounted while hidden (the globe behind the map, a kept page) must freeze their inputs and pause their render loops while hidden. Unigraph is not kept at all (`app/routes.ts › unloadsBehind`, read by `KeptPages` and a pane's warm tabs): behind, it is unmounted, and it saves on the way out what it needs to come back — the explorer's pending position write (`pendingSave`) and a running simulation's positions (`GpuGraph › finalKeep`, before the engine goes). A kept page (`app/KeptPages.tsx`) runs under its own frozen address and may not move the browser: what it navigates (a redirect route it sits on, a pane host restoring its tab) moves that frozen address only, and a move out of its section lets the page go.
- A stage that draws a camera over a scene (the engine's view) holds its picture still when its container changes size or place — a panel opening beside it, the sidebar folding: the camera moves by what the container's centre moved on the screen (`getBoundingClientRect` at the last resize against now), never a refit — and it draws inside the resize observer's callback, before the browser paints, so no frame shows a cleared buffer or the last picture stretched to the new box. The GPU engine's `onResize` is the rule's home.
- Anything a page can do gets a tool (see `assistant/AGENTS.md`) and a paragraph in its docs page (see `src/features/docs/AGENTS.md`).
